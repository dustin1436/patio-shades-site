Our Story

About Patio Shades DFW

Locally owned. Personally installed. Backed by Love Is Blinds.

DFW Owner

Durrell Glick

Durrell brings a craftsman's eye to every patio shade installation in DFW. Raised in a Mennonite community in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, he grew up around high-end construction — learning attention to detail, precision, and the value of doing a job right the first time.

He joined Love Is Blinds in April 2023 and quickly proved himself as one of the most reliable and consistent installers in the system. His work ethic and craftsmanship earned him the opportunity to take over the DFW territory, where he now serves homeowners across central and western Dallas–Fort Worth with his wife Alyssa and their three children.

When Durrell shows up, there's no subcontractor, no sales handoff, no runaround. He's the one who measures your space, discusses your options, builds your shade to spec, and installs it himself. That's the kind of personal, accountable service that's hard to find — and it's what sets Patio Shades DFW apart.

The Company

Backed by Love Is Blinds

Love Is Blinds is a growing franchise system with 12+ locations nationwide, specializing in custom blinds, shutters, and window treatments for homes and businesses. Patio Shades DFW is the exterior shade division of the DFW franchise — built on the same commitment to quality, fair pricing, and personal service.

That means Durrell isn't out here on his own. He has the backing of a franchise system with proven processes, supplier relationships with brands like SunPro, and a support team that helps ensure every customer gets a premium experience from start to finish.

When you hire Patio Shades DFW, you get the best of both worlds — a local owner who knows your neighborhood, and a national brand that stands behind the work.
